## Why This Order?

**Lena's access is the first security priority.** She can see the Admin menu despite being a Billing Clerk, but I haven't confirmed whether she can perform administrative actions. I would verify her actual permissions, review any privileged activity and contain any excessive access identified.

**The audience mismatch is the first technical fix.** It affects everyone and needs correcting so we can test sign-in and verify the other changes. The vendor's temporary workaround must also be removed.

**The account-matching issue follows.** Once sign-in is working, I would verify that Sam and his six colleagues can access their correct existing accounts.

This order protects against excessive access while allowing the SSO problems to be resolved and verified before launch.

<BR>

## Security Assessment

Not all three problems carry the same security risk  and the difference is in how each one fails.

The audience and account-matching failures prevent legitimate users from accessing Billing. That disrupts operations and threatens the launch, but neither reported failure shows that someone has gained unauthorised access.

The incorrect Admin role assignment is different. The application's broad group-name rule may grant users permissions beyond their roles. Lena's visible Admin menu raises that concern, although her ability to perform administrative actions still needs verification.

With the launch deadline approaching, there is pressure to make sign-in work quickly. These shortcuts would make the problem worse:

- Granting broad or elevated roles instead of defining explicit group-to-role mappings.

- Disabling audience validation or accepting any audience value.

- Linking users to the wrong accounts or creating duplicate accounts instead of fixing the identity mapping.

- Keeping the vendor's temporary workaround in place permanently.

**Making the application work should not come at the expense of verifying identity and enforcing the right access.**

<BR>

## Before Launch

- **Launch Recommendation:** Delay the launch until all three issues are fixed and testing confirms the Billing application is working securely.

- **Business Impact:** Launching with unresolved issues risks lost staff time, billing delays, unauthorised changes and additional recovery costs.

- **Required Testing:** Confirm users can sign in to their correct existing accounts, receive only the permissions appropriate to their roles and access the Billing application without the temporary audience workaround.

- **Final Decision:** If the issues cannot be resolved before the deadline, present the remaining risks and business impacts to the responsible decision-maker for formal approval before proceeding.
