
# 03. Fix Order & Verification Plan


With the SSO launch scheduled for tomorrow, I prioritised the fixes based on security risk, business impact, the number of users affected and dependencies between the problems.

| Priority | Problem | Recommended Fix | How to Verify |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lena receives incorrect Admin access | Replace the broad group-name rule with an explicit mapping to authorised Admin groups. | Confirm Lena can sign in and perform her Billing Clerk duties, but cannot access or perform Admin functions. |
| 2 | Invalid audience | Correct the Identifier (Entity ID) in Microsoft Entra ID to match the Billing application's expected value. Remove the vendor's temporary workaround. | Test SAML sign-in using the exact expected audience and confirm it succeeds without the workaround. |
| 3 | Sam and six colleagues receive `no matching account` | Configure the SAML NameID to send the email address the Billing application uses to identify accounts, after checking the affected users' attributes. | Confirm the affected users can sign in and reach their correct accounts. |

<br>

## Why This Order?

**Lena's access comes first** because it may expose administrative functions to someone who should not have them. I would verify the extent of her access and correct the role-mapping rule before launch.

**The audience mismatch comes next** because it affects sign-in more broadly. The permanent correction should replace the vendor's temporary workaround.

**The account-matching issue follows** because Sam and six colleagues still need to be able to access their correct accounts.

<br>

## Before Launch

I would recommend holding the launch until all three issues have been corrected and the relevant tests have passed.

The checks should confirm that legitimate users can reach their correct accounts, users receive only the permissions appropriate to their roles and sign-in works without the temporary audience workaround.

To business stakeholders, I would explain: “Delaying launch has a cost, but launching with these issues unresolved could add lost staff time, delayed billing and the cost of investigating and correcting any unauthorised changes. We would still need to fix the original problems, while also paying for the disruption they cause. Completing the fixes and checks before launch reduces that risk.”

If these conditions cannot be met before the deadline, I would present the unresolved issues and their business and security impacts to the accountable decision-maker for an explicit go-live decision, rather than weaken the checks simply to make sign-in work.
*This is a proposed fix and verification plan based on the supplied scenario. The changes have not yet been implemented or tested.*
