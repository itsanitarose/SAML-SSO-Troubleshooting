
# 01. Source Data

## How the Two Sides Are Set Up

### Our Identity Provider Sends

- **Audience (Entity ID):** `https://billing.vendor.example/saml/`
- **Reply URL:** `https://billing.vendor.example/saml/acs`
- **User identifier:** The user principal name (UPN), e.g. `sam.okoye@northwindhealth.example`
- **Groups:** The names of every group the user belongs to

### The Billing Application Expects

- **Audience (Entity ID):** `https://billing.vendor.example/saml`
- **Reply URL:** `https://billing.vendor.example/saml/acs`
- **User identifier:** The user's email address, e.g. `s.okoye@northwindhealth.example`
- **Roles:** Gives the Admin role to anyone in a group whose name contains `Billing`

## What Testers Reported

**Everyone**

The application shows "SSO error: invalid audience." Our identity provider logs show a successful sign-in every time.

The vendor has temporarily told the application to accept either version of the audience so testing can continue.

**Sam and Six Others Hired Since July**

They sign in at our identity provider, but the application says "no matching account."

Their UPNs differ from their email addresses because of the naming style introduced in July.

**Lena — Billing Clerk**

She signs in successfully but sees the Admin menu.

She belongs to the `Billing-Readers` group.
